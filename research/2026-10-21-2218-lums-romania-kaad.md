## LUMS National Outreach Programme
Catalog record: 8cf9de06-a379-4b6b-8617-c2a15712b195
Official source: https://nop.lums.edu.pk/how-to-apply-nop
The 2026–28 pipeline has a Summer Coaching Session application deadline of 23 February 2027 and supporting document deadline of 25 February 2027. Coaching is in July–August 2027, undergraduate admission applications are in January–February 2028, and classes begin September 2028. The 2027 deadline is specifically for the coaching stage, not the undergraduate application. Eligibility requires at least 80% in Matriculation or equivalent O-Level results and financial need. The page encourages students from all regions of Pakistan, rather than establishing universal international eligibility. Fully funded NOP scholarships require subsequent merit-based admission and financial review. Admission testing is required at a later stage, but no universal SAT, IELTS or TOEFL numeric threshold is given on this programme page.
Current catalog record has no deadline or cycle and describes the programme as intended for international students. These fields merit review.

## Romania MFA scholarship
Catalog record: d33cdd8d-4051-4c17-abf7-9f04bf5d8936
Official sources: https://studyinromania.gov.ro/mfa_2026-2027 and https://scholarships.studyinromania.gov.ro/scholarship-about
The confirmed 2026/27 application window was 16 February–31 March 2026. A 2027/28 deadline was not confirmed. Eligible applicants are non-EU citizens subject to specified exclusions, with minimum grades equivalent to 7/10 Romanian scale. Medicine, dentistry and pharmacy are excluded. Bachelor's and Master's courses are in Romanian; PhD courses can use other approved languages. Funding covers tuition, a monthly scholarship and dormitory support subject to availability and subsidy limits. No universal IELTS, TOEFL or SAT minimum is specified in scholarship rules.
The current catalog nationality field is unrestricted and the description omits significant conditions.

## KAAD scholarships
Catalog record: 32bdbc52-bdfa-4b27-a16a-59a2d8e71d46
Official sources: https://www.kaad.de/en/stipendien/stipendienprogramm-1 and https://www.kaad.de/en/stipendien/stipendienprogramm-2
KAAD Programme 1 targets qualified postgraduate applicants currently living in eligible developing or emerging countries. Programme 2 supports eligible students already in Germany and is accessed through Catholic university chaplaincies. Both impose additional religious/community and German-language requirements, typically B1 or A2 for English-taught courses. No single deadline or universal EUR 861 monthly award was established from these official pages. IELTS, TOEFL and SAT thresholds are not given as universal KAAD conditions.
The generic catalog record combines different schemes and should undergo identity reconciliation before any correction.
