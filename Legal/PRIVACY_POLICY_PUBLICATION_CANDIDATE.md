This Privacy Policy explains what information EduT collects, why and how it is used, with whom it may be shared, and what choices you have. External scholarship websites and services have their own privacy practices.

## 1. What information we collect

We collect information that you provide, information created when you use EduT, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service. Depending on how you use EduT, this can include:

- **Account and contact details:** email address, account identifier, name or display name, and any optional contact information. Authentication credentials are handled by the authentication provider; the app should not expose your password in user-facing profiles.
- **Student and scholarship profile:** education history, intended level or degree, preferred countries and majors, academic grades or GPA, language test scores, scholarship preferences, nationality or country information you enter, and optional household or family-income information used for matching.
- **Application activity:** scholarships you view or save, shortlists, application stages, goals, notes, uploaded application documents, and other content you enter.
- **Community content:** posts, messages, uploaded images and videos, reports, blocks, and interactions with other users.
- **Advisor and consultation content:** submitted professional profile, biographies, experience, credentials, specialties, photo, external profile and introduction-video links, approval status, student-advisor assignments, consultation requests, service selections, contact details you choose to provide, schedules, and records relating to consultation or payment status.
- **Device and technical data:** application or device identifiers used for push notifications, sign-in/session information, configuration preferences, and service or security logs. Technical service providers may also receive IP addresses and connection information when you access their systems.
- **Communications:** support requests, reported safety issues, and messages you send us.

We do not need you to provide government identity documents, passwords, payment card details, or bank credentials in community chat. Please do not post these in public areas.

## 2. How information is used

We process information to:
1. Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure your account.
2. Personalize scholarship discovery, matching, recommendations, and application planning.
3. Let you save opportunities and manage your application activities.
4. Enable community posts, messaging, reporting, and blocking.
5. Review advisor applications; display approved professional profiles; manage student-advisor connections and consultations.
6. Coordinate requested services and keep records of payments, refunds, or disputes where relevant.
7. Deliver notifications and respond to support and privacy requests.
8. Investigate misuse, secure the platform, troubleshoot failures, and comply with applicable obligations.
9. Understand service operation and improve functionality using information collected for that purpose.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a lawful basis, processing may be necessary to provide the service you request, comply with legal obligations, protect legitimate security and operational interests, or rely on your consent for specific optional processing. Available lawful bases and rights depend on where you live. Any optional advertising or other tracking requiring permission must not be activated without the required choices and notices.

## 3. Who can see information

Your academic and financial profile information, private scholarship notes, and saved application information are intended to be accessible through your own account, not published in public community profiles.

Community information that you choose to post may be visible to other users. An approved advisor's professional profile, profile photo, biography, qualifications, languages, specialties, and introductory materials may be publicly discoverable within EduT. A consultation or conversation can expose information to its participants, including contact details when the relevant workflow permits it. Other users may retain copies of content you choose to share.

Avoid sharing sensitive information beyond what is needed for scholarship or advisory services.

## 4. Service providers and disclosures

EduT uses **Supabase** for services including authentication, database operations, and file storage. The iOS project also integrates **LiveKit** functionality for real-time advisor communications. Apple services may be used for distribution, app notifications, and device functionality. When such services process information, they do so for operating the relevant feature and under their applicable terms.

We may share the information required for a feature with:
- Other users and advisors, as described above, where you choose to participate or a requested service requires it.
- Providers that host or protect the app, deliver authentication, store files, provide communication or notification functions, or process services.
- Payment or transaction providers, **if a paid feature is used**, according to the actual checkout arrangement; do not enter payment card details in messages.
- Authorities or other parties where disclosure is legally required, needed to address fraud/security incidents, or otherwise legally permitted.

EduT may link to third-party scholarship providers, social platforms, external video providers, WhatsApp or email applications. Opening these services can disclose information to their operators and is governed by their own privacy notices.

**Provider verification before publication:** verify the actual hosted services, payment processors, any analytics and crash-reporting SDKs, and whether additional vendors receive data. Do not publish a categorical claim that data is never shared, sold, or processed overseas unless independently confirmed.

## 5. Storage, international processing, and security

The app uses remote infrastructure to authenticate accounts and store the information required by its features. Your information may be processed outside your country of residence, depending on the location of our infrastructure, communication providers, and other recipients.

We use account access restrictions and other security measures appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You should protect your credentials, use a strong password, and notify us of suspected unauthorized use. Permission to access photos, camera, microphone, or notifications is controlled through the device and only needed for the relevant feature.

## 6. Retention and account deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain your account and requested features, resolve disputes, maintain service security, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention may differ for user content, consultation and transaction records, security logs, and provider backups. We do not promise an unverified fixed deletion or backup-purge deadline.

You can request permanent account deletion in **Profile → Settings → Delete account**, subject to the feature's availability in your current app version. The deletion process is designed to remove your authentication account and associated user-owned data and files. Limited information may remain where required by law, for resolving disputes or abuse investigations, or temporarily in system backups; information other users independently copied or lawfully retain may also remain outside our control.

Withdrawing from an **advisor role** is a separate operation from deleting your EduT account and does not, by itself, delete the account. If you cannot access the in-app deletion option, email the privacy contact above.

## 7. Your controls and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or request deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent for processing based on consent. You may also have the right to complain to an appropriate data protection authority.

You can edit supported profile details in the app, change certain device permissions through iOS settings, block or report users, and request account deletion. To exercise other rights, email the privacy contact provided above. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account before acting on a request. We will respond within timelines applicable to your request.

## 8. Notifications, cookies and tracking

The mobile app can store sign-in/session information and preferences necessary to work. Native app storage is not automatically equivalent to browser cookies. When you visit scholarship websites, hosted forms, videos, or other external services, they may use cookies or similar technologies under their own notices.

EduT's current iOS privacy manifest declares that the app does **not** engage in cross-app tracking. If EduT later implements advertising identifiers or optional tracking that legally requires opt-in permission, it must disclose the new practice and provide the required controls **before enabling it**.

## 9. Children and minors

EduT is designed to help prospective students, including people who may be under the age of majority in some countries. Age and parental-consent requirements vary by jurisdiction. **Before publication, the Operator must confirm the intended minimum age, whether underage users can register, and enforce any necessary age or parental authorization safeguards.** Users must not provide another person's sensitive information without permission.
