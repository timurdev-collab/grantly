## Proposal A: Global Citizens Scholarship — Aberystwyth University (2027/28)
- **Action proposed:** new private draft, not published. Official programme: https://www.aber.ac.uk/en/study-with-us/international/scholarships/global-citizens-scholarship/
- Degree levels: Bachelor (including foundation), taught Master, research Master/MPhil and PhD subject to course eligibility.
- Tuition-fee discount **per year**: undergraduate humanities £3,000; undergraduate sciences £4,500; taught Master humanities £3,000; taught Master sciences £3,800; MBA £3,000; postgraduate research humanities £3,000 and sciences £3,800. DProf amount is TBC. These are tuition discounts, **not cash stipends**.
- Applicant must be a **new student starting 2027/28**, classified as an international tuition-fee payer, with an eligible course offer and an application for the course **by 1 August**. The 2027 calendar year is inferred from the 2027/28 intake and must be recorded as cycle-context, not falsely described as a separately printed date.
- Automatic assessment with degree application; **no separate scholarship application**. Exclusions: home-fee status, external funding, veterinary science, nursing, distance-learning and overseas-delivered courses. Renewed annual discount depends on satisfactory progress and continuing fee eligibility.
- Can be combined with a **separate** International Accommodation Award, subject to its own availability and requirements. Do **not** set scholarship `accommodation=true` as unconditional or claim guaranteed free housing. Source: https://www.aber.ac.uk/en/study-with-us/international/inclusive-accommodation/
- Nationality: no fixed country whitelist established; **international-fee liability**, not `eligible_nationalities=['ALL']` interpreted as unrestricted access.
- English: **admission-level**, varies by course and exemptions. No scholarship-specific universal IELTS/TOEFL minimum or SAT requirement established. See https://www.aber.ac.uk/en/study-with-us/international/english-requirements/

## Proposal B: Aber Grad Global Scholarship — Aberystwyth University (2027/28)
- **Action proposed:** distinct private draft, not a duplicate of Global Citizens. Official programme: https://www.aber.ac.uk/en/study-with-us/international/scholarships/abergrad-international-scholarship/
- £2,000 **annual tuition-fee reduction** for qualifying 2027 Aberystwyth undergraduate graduates entering eligible postgraduate study in 2027/28. No living stipend.
- Applicant must hold at least a UK 2:2 Aberystwyth Bachelor's degree, be an international fee payer, have a qualifying postgraduate offer and apply **directly to the university (not through an agent)** by **31 July 2027**.
- Eligible: taught Master's, MRes, MPhil, LLM by Research and PhD; the page also specifies a list of eligible distance-learning programmes despite its general campus-based language. Preserve this nuance, do not impose an absolute distance-learning exclusion.
- Excludes staff candidates and external scholarship funding (loans excepted). Can combine with Global Citizens and the separate accommodation award subject to conditions and an overall cap on combined Master's tuition discounts.
- No separate scholarship application; automatic assessment from academic application. No scholarship-specific IELTS/TOEFL/SAT score stated; course admission requirements apply.
- Provenance proposals: `title`, `application_cycle`, `deadline` (2027-07-31 **course application deadline**, not separate scholarship form), `tuition_coverage`, `degree_levels`, `description`, `deadline_notes`.

## Programme-level language testing: keep conditional, not global
